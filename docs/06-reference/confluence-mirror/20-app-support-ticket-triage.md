A guide to help teams filter out noise before escalating to App Support, covering incidents,
hotfixes and expected system behaviours that do not require an App Support ticket. Triage
answers:

- Is this a **known incident** already being handled by another team?
- Is this a **hotfix** or deployment side effect that engineering owns?
- Is this **expected behaviour** from a feature, not actually a bug?
- Is this a **misrouted report** that belongs to a different team?

Triage page 2909765695, July Ticket Triage page 3022389323) and in the Cover Runbook.
