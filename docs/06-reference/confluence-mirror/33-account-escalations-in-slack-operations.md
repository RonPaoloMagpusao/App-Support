This is the Operations escalation matrix. Useful to App Support because it explains where calls
that are not tech issues go, and because it sets the compliance rules for what can be posted in
Slack, which apply to App Support threads too.

Escalations are posted in the **`mme_solutions`** Slack channel (examples in the page use
`#mme-solutions`).

## How to escalate

1. Attempt to solve the enquiry yourself: check Notes, Communications and other relevant pages
   in the customer's Horizon profile.
2. If unable to resolve, tell the caller you need to place them on hold.
3. With the customer on hold, post a brief summary in Slack and tag the appropriate group. If
   about 2 minutes pass with no help, take the customer off hold, apologise, and offer a call
   back or continued hold.
4. Once an agent from the group confirms they can assist, transfer the call.
5. **Compliance team note:** if a caller asks to speak to the case manager or a compliance team
   member, advise that you will follow it up and we will reach out. Gather as much information
   as possible, or advise them to reply to the email Compliance sent. **Do not promise a call
   back**; Compliance usually responds by email.

## Compliance requirements for Slack

MoneyMe does not have complete control over Slack's security features, and cannot guarantee
deletion of data, which is an obligation under the Privacy Act.

**Can be shared on Slack:**
- Customer's full name
- Their contact number
- Loan reference or ID number (`10001######` or `LAI-000#####`)

**Cannot be shared on Slack:**
- Residential address or place of employment
- Date of birth
- Any government issued documentation, such as driver's licence or passport details

Escalation message examples use a Horizon application note link rather than customer detail, for
example `https://horizon.moneyme.com.au/Note/ApplicationNotes/1000*****`.
