| Urgency | Definition | Time to response | Time to resolution | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Major incident with very high impact. Needs to be resolved quickly to avoid further problems | **4 hours** | **6 hours** (from acknowledgement) | A customer or partner facing issue that affects or may affect all customers/partners. Something wrong with the website so customers cannot access it, or the payment portal is not working and we cannot process customer payments |
| **High** | Major incident with significant impact | **6 hours** | **8 hours** | A customer or partner facing issue affecting a subset of customers/partners. A few customers cannot access the website while it works for others |
| **Medium** | Incident with low impact | **40 hours** | **48 hours** (2 days) | An issue that gives minor inconvenience to customers/partners |
| **Low** | Bugs or support issues that do not impact product usability. Can be marked Low if a temporary fix or workaround is available | **240 hours** | **240 hours** (10 days) | An issue that does not affect product usability; misspelled words in the UI; wrong placement of logos or text; a data fix affecting only one or a few customers with no high impact |
